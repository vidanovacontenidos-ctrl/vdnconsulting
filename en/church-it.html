Questions

We are a small congregation. Is this for us?+

Under a hundred people with one service a week, a one-off project with templates and recorded training usually serves you better than a monthly retainer. We say so at the walkthrough even when it means not selling the recurring service.

Do we have to change the tools we already use?+

No. Solo recomendamos cambiar algo cuando esa herramienta es la causa concreta de un problema, y con los números a la vista.

Do you work with any denomination?+

Yes. We make sure the network holds, the stream stays up, the pieces are consistent and the congregation data is protected.

How do you handle people data?+

The same way we handle an accounting firm’s: role-based access, mandatory multi-factor, verified backups and a record of who accessed what. Minor records and background checks are held separately with restricted access.

Do you use AI for content?+

For speeding up repetitive work, yes, and we always say so. A tool can imitate a voice, it cannot invent one: that comes from theology, people and each community’s history, and it gets decided with leadership.
