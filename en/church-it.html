Questions

We are a small congregation. Is this for us?+

Depends what you need. Under a hundred people with one service a week, a one-off project with templates and recorded training usually serves you better than a monthly retainer. We say so at the walkthrough even when it means not selling the recurring service.

Do we have to change the tools we already use?+

No. We work across lo que hay. Si usan Planning Center, ProPresenter, Tithe.ly, Subsplash o cualquier combinación, nos adaptamos. Solo recomendamos cambiar algo cuando esa herramienta es la causa concreta de un problema, y con los números a la vista.

Do you work with any denomination?+

Yes. Our work is technical and creative. We do not weigh in on doctrine, liturgy or content. We make sure the network holds, the sound carries, the pieces are consistent and the congregation data is protected.

How do you handle people data?+

The same way we handle an accounting firm’s: role-based access, mandatory multi-factor, verified backups and a record of who accessed what. Minor records and background checks are held separately with restricted access.

Do you use AI for content?+

For speeding up repetitive work, yes, and we always say so. For defining the ministry’s voice, no. A tool can imitate a voice, it cannot invent one: that comes from theology, people and each community’s history, and it gets decided with leadership.
