Sustain

Somebody has to answer when email stops arriving at 8am on a Monday. Somebody has to know whether last night’s backup actually ran. That is this unit: the one that lets the rest of your organization ignore infrastructure entirely.

Book an assessment See response times

When you need it

Three signs you already need this.

“Who do we call?”

When something breaks, the answer depends on who happens to be in the office. There is no number, there is a favor.

“Backups are running, I think.”

There is an external drive plugged in somewhere and nobody has ever restored a file from it. That is not a backup, it is a hope.

“She still has her mailbox.”

Someone left months ago and their account is still active, still in the shared drive, still on the license bill.

Questions

Do you replace our internal IT person?+

Usually we free them from firefighting so they can work on what they know about your business. We take standby, monitoring and documentation; they stay the internal face.

What if the problem is our internet provider?+

We open the ticket, chase it and keep you informed. Part of what you are buying is somebody pursuing that for you.

How do we know what you did all month?+

You have permanent dashboard access to every ticket, who took it and how long it ran. We also send a one-page monthly summary: what happened, what got fixed, what deserves attention.

Do you carry cyber liability insurance?+

Yes, and we send the certificate before you sign anything. A surprising number change the subject.
