What we hear

Three sentences we hear in every room.

"The person who knew it left for college."

The volunteer who ran the booth stopped coming and nothing was written down. Every Sunday somebody relearns it live, in front of everyone.

"The stream dropped and we didn't know why."

They found out from a text sent by someone watching at home. Nobody was monitoring the signal.

"New people came and we never heard from them again."

They filled out a card, the card went in a box, and by the time anyone read it three weeks had passed.

Questions

We're a small church on a tight budget. Is this for us?+

Depends what you need. Under a hundred people with one service a week, a one-time cleanup plus recorded training usually serves you better than a monthly agreement. We'll say so during the walkthrough even when it means not selling you the recurring service.

Do we have to switch the software we already use?+

No. We work with what you have. Planning Center, ProPresenter, Tithe.ly, Subsplash, any combination — we adapt. We only recommend changing a tool when it's the concrete cause of a problem, and we show you the numbers first.

Do you work with any denomination?+

Yes. Our work is technical. We don't weigh in on doctrine, liturgy or content. We make sure the network holds, the stream doesn't drop and your congregation's data is protected.

How do you handle congregation data?+

The same way we'd handle an accounting firm's: role-based access, mandatory multi-factor, verified backups and a record of who accessed what. Minor data and background-check records are held separately with restricted access.

Can you support a multi-site church?+

Yes, and it's where this unit earns the most. Content sync between campuses, a consistent booth setup so a volunteer can serve at any location, and one dashboard covering every site's stream health.
