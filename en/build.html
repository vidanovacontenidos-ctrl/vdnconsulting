Questions

Why are you cheaper than an agency?+

Because the website is not our main business, it is one unit among five. We do not carry a creative department at agency overhead. If you want a brand campaign with video production, an agency will do it better and we will say so.

What happens after handover?+

You get the revisions included in your scope. After that you can walk away, since the site is yours and it works, or add monthly maintenance covering updates, backups, uptime monitoring and change hours.

Do you work in WordPress?+

Depends on the case. For sites with a blog and frequent editing, yes, because your team can actually run it. For campaign landing pages and systems, no. It is heavy and brings security maintenance you do not need. We tell you which we are choosing and why before we start.

What about ranking on Google?+

The Commercial scope includes the technical foundation and content structure. That puts you in a position to rank, but ranking seriously is sustained monthly work, not something handed over with a site. If you want that, it is contracted separately with clear expectations.
