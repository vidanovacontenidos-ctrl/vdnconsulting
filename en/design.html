The method

Five stages, each approved before the next begins.

Nobody redoes a logo three times because the designer missed. It gets redone because drawing started before deciding what to say.

Questions

We already have a logo and do not want to change it. Does this still apply?+

Yes, and it is a common case. Often the logo is fine and the problem is that nothing was ever built around it.

Why is brand design charged separately from the site?+

Because they are different jobs and one governs the other. You can buy the site alone, but we will tell you what gets given up.

How many rounds of revisions are included?+

In practice that is enough because each stage is approved before the next: by the time you reach the logo we have already agreed direction, palette and tone. Extra rounds are quoted hourly and flagged in advance.

Is the design made by a person or by software?+

We use generative tools to explore directions quickly during Territory, and we always say so. The final mark is built in vectors, by hand, because it has to work at sixteen pixels and a generated image does not solve that.

Do you work with non-profits?+

Yes: foundations, associations, schools and faith communities. They tend to share the same picture — serious operation, dated image, manual processes — and the same underlying problem: the image signals less than the organization delivers.
