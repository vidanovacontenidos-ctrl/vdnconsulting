Common questions

We already have someone who handles our computers. Why would we need you?+

If it works today, keep it. The gap shows up when that person is out sick, leaves, or when the problem is the network rather than a single machine. We run on documentation and standby, so there's never one head holding all the credentials. Often we work alongside an internal tech and cover what they can't reach.

What happens to the equipment we already own?+

It stays. We don't sell hardware to replace things that work. During the assessment we separate what's fine, what needs updating and what should be switched off. The first savings usually come from cancelling licenses and servers nobody uses anymore.

Do we have to sign an annual contract?+

No. Billing is monthly with 30 days' notice to cancel. If we part ways, you receive complete documentation, credentials and access. Retaining clients with a contract instead of with service strikes us as the wrong way to run this.

We're small. Does this make sense for us?+

From about five seats it starts to pay off, especially if you handle client data or payments. Below that a one-time cleanup usually serves you better than a monthly agreement. We'll tell you that during the assessment even when it means not selling you anything.

Are you actually local to us?+

Monitoring, help desk, identity and automation are remote and nationwide. For hands-on work — cabling, cameras, rack installs — we coordinate a vetted local technician and we direct and document the work. Say where you are and we'll tell you honestly what that looks like.

Do you support Spanish-speaking teams?+

Yes, natively. Documentation, training and live support in both English and Spanish, with no interpreter in the middle. For organizations with bilingual staff or volunteers this tends to be the deciding factor.
